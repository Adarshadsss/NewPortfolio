Orchid Memory - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

Important: Orchid Memory is a casual memory matching game. You do not need to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play the game.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Orchid Memory ("the Game"), published by AdsssVerse ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information when you use the Game.

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about information that may be processed through the Game and third-party services used to provide advertising and related functionality.

2. Information We Collect

Orchid Memory itself does not require users to create an account and does not directly ask users to provide personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Postal address
  • Password

However, third-party services integrated into the Game, such as advertising services, may automatically process certain technical or device-related information.

3. Advertising

Orchid Memory may display advertisements using third-party advertising services, including Unity Ads.

Advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, approximate location, advertising-related information, and information about interactions with advertisements.

This information may be used for purposes including advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, compliance, and improving advertising functionality.

Unity Ads may collect and share certain information according to its own privacy practices and the configuration of the advertising service used by the Game.

4. Device and Technical Information

Third-party services used by the Game may automatically process technical information required to provide advertising and related services.

Depending on the services and configuration, this may include:

  • Device or advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location
  • Device information
  • App and advertising interaction information
  • Technical diagnostics
  • Information required to prevent fraud and maintain security

5. How Information Is Used

Information processed through third-party services may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and displaying advertisements
  • Measuring advertising performance
  • Analytics and service improvement
  • Fraud prevention and security
  • Compliance with applicable laws and policies
  • Providing and maintaining Game functionality

6. Third-Party Services

Orchid Memory may use third-party services to provide advertising and other functionality.

Unity Ads

Unity Ads is an advertising service provided by Unity Technologies. Unity Ads may collect and process certain device and advertising information as described in its privacy documentation.

For more information about Unity's privacy practices, please visit Unity's official privacy policy.

Unity Privacy Policy

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information processed by third-party advertising services may be shared with those service providers and their partners as required to provide advertising, analytics, security, fraud prevention, and related services.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled through the Game and its services.

Third-party services may use security measures such as encryption in transit and other technical safeguards when processing data.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Orchid Memory does not maintain a user account system and therefore does not maintain an account profile containing your personal information.

Information processed by third-party services may be retained according to the policies and retention practices of those services.

Where a third-party service provides a mechanism for requesting deletion of information, users may use the applicable mechanism provided by that service.

10. Children's Privacy

Orchid Memory is a casual game and does not require users to create an account or submit personal information.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Advertising and third-party services used by the Game may have their own requirements and safeguards relating to children and age-based advertising.

11. Permissions

Orchid Memory may request permissions required for the Game or its integrated services to function.

We do not intentionally request access to contacts, messages, photos, microphone, or other personal content unless such access is specifically required and disclosed for a future version of the Game.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Game, third-party services, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Orchid Memory, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the developer's official website.
